title=Railroader: Wayman

description=[h2]Build your railway[/h2]
description=Expand Railroader's railway with buildable tracks, crossings, curves and turnouts in Project Zomboid Build 42. Connect your routes to the existing network and use them with Railroader trains, in singleplayer or multiplayer.
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Straight and diagonal track, curves with or without sleepers and several turnout designs, including three-way turnouts.
description=[*]Construction guides with connection feedback and distances to help align your pieces with Wayman, Railroader and other mods' route endpoints.
description=[*]A route editor to connect built pieces and configure turnouts, with saved drafts so you can continue later.
description=[*]An interactive World Map overlay to view your routes and select their connections, with numbered nodes while editing.
description=[*]Construction recipes with material and skill requirements, railroad spike forging and casting, and material recovery when disassembling pieces.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]How it works[/h2]
description=Build your railway pieces using the guides, then open the Graph Editor with [b].[/b] (configurable through key bindings menu) or the [b]G[/b] button on the World Map. Connect their nodes into routes, configure the turnouts and press [b]Accept[/b] to make the routes available to trains. Placing track pieces alone does not create a drivable route.
description=
description=[h2]Requirements[/h2]
description=Project Zomboid [b]Build 42[/b] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3774360904]Railroader 3.0[/url]. Enable both mods.
description=
